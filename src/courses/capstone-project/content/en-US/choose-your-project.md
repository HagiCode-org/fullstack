This is the final project of the series. You build one small app of your own, from an empty folder to something other people can open. In this lesson you choose what to build and make it small enough to finish. You finish with a project name and a one-sentence goal.

## What you will do

1. Read the four suggested projects.

   **Resume maker.** A page where a person fills in their details and gets a clean resume they can save.

   **Notes app.** A place to write, find and delete short notes that are still there the next day.

   **Task board.** Columns such as "to do", "doing" and "done", with cards you can move between them.

   **Q&A helper.** A page where a person asks a question and gets an answer based on text you provide.

2. Pick one, or describe your own idea. Your own idea is fine if you can say who uses it and what they do with it in two sentences.
3. Make it small. Ask yourself what the smallest version is that still helps one real person. Cut everything else for now. A finished small app is better than an unfinished large one.
4. Name the one person you are building for, even if it is only yourself.
5. Ask your AI assistant: "Here is my project idea. Tell me what is too big for a first version, and what I could leave for later." Read its answer and remove what is too big.
6. Create a new project on GitHub for it, as you did earlier in the series, and write the one-sentence goal at the top of its description.

## Check yourself

- You chose one project and can explain it in one sentence.
- You named the person who will use it.
- You wrote down at least three things you will not build in the first version.
- The project exists on GitHub with its goal written down.
