You have built and launched an app with an AI assistant. In this last lesson you look back at the project and turn what you learned into a checklist for the next one. You finish with a review checklist in your own words.

## What you will do

1. Open the history of your project on GitHub and read the list of save points and pull requests from top to bottom. It tells the story of how the app was built.
2. Write **What the AI did well**: three things that went faster or better than you expected, such as drafting a page or explaining an error.
3. Write **What I had to catch**: the problems you found by checking, and which check found each one. For example, a change the assistant made that you did not ask for, a wrong answer, or a secret in the wrong place.
4. Write **What I would do differently**: one change to how you wrote the spec, how you asked or how you checked.
5. Turn the second and third lists into a **review checklist** for the next project, ten lines or fewer. Each line should be a question you can answer yes or no. For example: "Did I read every change before accepting it?" and "Is there a spending limit on every paid service?"
6. Save the checklist in a file you will reuse, such as a template you copy into every new project.
7. Decide what to build next. Pick something a little bigger than this project, and write its first one-sentence goal.

## Check yourself

- You read the full history of your project.
- Your checklist has ten lines or fewer, and each can be answered yes or no.
- You can name at least one thing the AI got wrong that you caught.
- You wrote the goal of your next project.
