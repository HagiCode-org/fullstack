A **feature brief** is a short written description of what the feature must do and must not do. Your AI assistant builds from it, and you check the result against it. In this lesson you write the brief for your AI feature. You finish with one page you can paste into your assistant.

## What you will do

1. Open a new note or file in your project and name it `feature-brief`.
2. Write **Who uses it**: one sentence about the person who will use the feature.
3. Write **What it does**: the input the person gives, and the output they should get. Give one real example of each.
4. Write **What it must never do**. This part matters most for an AI feature. Examples: never show a made-up fact as if it were true, never send private text to anyone other than the model service you chose, never keep running without a stop when it fails, never charge more than the limit you set. Write at least three rules for your own feature.
5. Write **What happens on failure**: the message the person sees if the model gives no answer or the service cannot be reached.
6. Write **How we will know it works**: three or more test inputs, each with the answer you would accept. You will use these in the lesson "Judge the output".
7. Ask your AI assistant: "Read my feature brief. List anything unclear, missing or in conflict." Fix the brief where the points are fair.
8. Save a checkpoint of the brief in your project, as you learned in the earlier courses.

## Check yourself

- The brief names the person, the input, the output and one real example.
- The brief lists at least three things the feature must never do.
- The brief says what the person sees when something fails.
- You have at least three test inputs with the answers you would accept.
