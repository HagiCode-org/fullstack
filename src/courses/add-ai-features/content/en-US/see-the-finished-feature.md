In this course you add one AI feature to a small app. An **AI feature** is a part of an app where a computer program called a **model** reads some text and writes a useful answer. In this lesson you only look at a finished example and decide which feature you want to build. You finish with one sentence that describes your feature.

Three kinds of feature are common in small apps. **Summarising** turns a long text into a few lines. **Answering questions** lets a person ask something about their own notes or documents. **Classifying** sorts items into groups, such as marking each message as urgent or not urgent.

## What you will do

1. Open the finished example your course provides. It is a small app with one AI feature inside it.
2. Use the feature at least three times with different text. Write down what you typed and what came back.
3. Note one answer that was good and one that was weak or wrong. Every AI feature has both, and you will learn to measure this later in the course.
4. Choose which of the three kinds of feature you want to add: summarising, answering questions or classifying.
5. Choose which of your earlier apps will receive the feature. A small app that already works is the best choice.
6. Ask your AI assistant to explain in plain words how the example uses the model, and read the answer. You do not need to understand every detail, only the idea: the app sends text to the model and shows what comes back.

**Note:** model services are not available in every country or region. Before you go on, check that you can reach a service you are allowed to use. If your usual choice is blocked where you live, pick another one now.

## Check yourself

- You used the finished example and wrote down at least one good and one weak answer.
- You can say your feature in one sentence, for example "Summarise each note in two lines".
- You know which earlier app will receive the feature.
- You have a model service you can reach from your computer.
