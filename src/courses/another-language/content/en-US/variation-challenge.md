For the last lesson you go one step further. You ask the AI to do the same job in a third language and judge the result on your own. You have seen that the method does not depend on the language, and here you prove it with a language you choose.

## What you will do

1. Choose a third language. Ask the AI to suggest three that suit your job and explain in two sentences what each is usually used for. Pick one.
2. Save a checkpoint so that your earlier work is safe.
3. Reuse your brief. Change only the sentence that names the language, and keep your finish checklist as it was.
4. Ask the AI for the first version, the exact steps to run it and a list of what must be installed first.
5. Install and run it. Hand over every error text you meet, and go back to the checkpoint if three attempts fail.
6. Test it with the same three inputs as before, including the unusual one.
7. Put all versions through your finish checklist and compare them.
8. Ask the AI which version it would choose for this job and why, then decide for yourself.
9. Write a short note for your future self: when would you choose each language for a small job, and what mattered more than the language?

**Safety note.** Test any script that touches files on a copy first. Do not give any program access to personal accounts or passwords for this exercise.

## Check yourself

- The third version passes the same finish checklist or you know exactly why it does not.
- You compared the results using the same inputs.
- You wrote down what mattered more than the language.
