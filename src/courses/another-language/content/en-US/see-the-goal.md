So far the AI chose the tools for you. In this course you ask it to do a job in **Python**, a programming language you have probably never used. You will either rebuild a small app you made earlier or write an automation script, which is a short program that does a repeated chore for you, such as renaming files or collecting information.

The point is not to learn Python. The point is to see that you can work with an AI in a language you do not know, and to learn how to judge the result without reading every line.

## What you will do

1. Choose the job. Two good options are rebuilding the small app from an earlier course, or a script that does one chore you really do, for example tidying the files in a folder.
2. Write down what the job must do, in your own words, before you mention Python at all. This description will not change between languages.
3. Write down how you will check that the job is done. For example "the folder has no loose files left" or "the app shows the same entries".
4. Decide what you want to learn from the comparison: how long it takes, how much you had to explain and whether the result behaves the same.
5. Make a note of how you feel about working in an unknown language, so that you can compare after the last lesson.

## Check yourself

- You can describe the job without naming any language.
- You have a short way to check that the job is done.
- You know which of the two options you chose.
