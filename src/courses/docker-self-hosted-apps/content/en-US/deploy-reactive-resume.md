Reactive Resume is a free, open-source program for building resumes in your browser. It is made of several programs that work together, such as the web app itself and a database, so it is a realistic first example of a multi-container app. In this lesson you will start it on your own computer and open it in your browser. At the end you will have a working copy running in Docker.

Setup steps change as the project changes. Always follow the project's current official instructions, not this page and not an old tutorial. Your AI helper must read those instructions too.

## What you will do

1. Open the official Reactive Resume documentation and find its Docker setup page. Copy the link.
2. Give that link, or the text of the page, to your AI helper and ask: "Read these official instructions. Tell me, step by step, what I need to do on my computer. Do not guess any setting that the page does not mention."
3. Create a new empty folder for the app, so its files stay together and separate from your other projects.
4. Ask the AI to explain every step before you run it, using the method from the last lesson: ports, where data lives, passwords, how to back up.
5. Start the app by following the official steps, then open the address the instructions give you in your browser.
6. Create an account in your own copy and make a test resume with made-up information.

**Safety note:** use fake details in this lesson. You will protect real data in the next two lessons.

## Check yourself

- The app opens in your browser at an address on your own computer.
- You can see all of its containers running in Docker.
- You made a test resume and it is still there after you refresh the page.
