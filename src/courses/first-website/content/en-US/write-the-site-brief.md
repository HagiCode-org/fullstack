A brief is the written request the assistant builds from. In this lesson you write the brief for your homepage using the five-part template from the course **Build with an AI Partner**. By the end you will have a brief the assistant has already asked questions about.

## The five parts, filled in for a homepage

1. **Goal.** For example: a one-page personal site that introduces me and shows three projects.
2. **Who uses it.** For example: classmates, friends and people who may want to contact me.
3. **What it does.** A header with my name, an introduction, a projects section with three entries, and a contact line.
4. **What it must not do.** No pop-ups, no background music, no forms that collect other people's information.
5. **How I will know it is done.** For example: the page opens from the folder, shows three projects, and every link works.

## Add a few choices of your own

Describe the feel in plain words, such as calm, bright or playful. Name one or two colours. Say whether you want a light or dark look. You do not need to name a framework. A framework is a ready-made set of building blocks for making sites. The assistant may choose one, such as Astro, and that is fine as long as it explains what it chose and how to open the site.

## What you will do

1. Create a file named `brief.md` in your `first-website` folder.
2. Fill in the five parts, using the example above as a guide but your own details.
3. Add your three project titles with a one-line description for each.
4. Ask the assistant: `Before you start, list any questions you have about this brief.`
5. Answer the questions and write the answers into `brief.md`.

## Check yourself

- Your brief has five parts and each is specific.
- The "done" part can be checked by clicking and looking.
- The assistant's questions are answered inside the brief.
