The first version is the assistant's first attempt at your brief. In this lesson you ask for it, open it and record what is wrong. By the end you will have a homepage on your screen and a list of improvements.

## What you will do

1. Open your `first-website` folder in the assistant.
2. Paste your brief and add: `Build the first version. Keep it simple. Tell me how to open the site in my browser, and list the files you created.`
3. When the assistant finishes, read its summary. If it chose a framework, note its name and how it says to start the site.
4. Open the site in your browser by following the assistant's instructions.
5. Look at the whole page slowly. Check the name, the introduction, the three projects, the contact line and the colours.
6. Write a list of what is wrong or missing, one item per line, and put the most important first.
7. Compare the site with the "done" part of your brief and mark which checks already pass.
8. Open GitHub Desktop, make your first commit with the description `First version`.

Do not ask for fixes yet. A complete list shows you what matters most, and you will work through it in the next lessons.

## If the site does not open

Copy the exact error text and the steps you followed, and paste them to the assistant. Use the routine from **When something breaks** in the course **Build with an AI Partner**: paste the error exactly, say what you expected, and stop after three failed tries.

## Check yourself

- The site opens in your browser.
- You have a written list of problems with the most important first.
- A commit named `First version` appears in your GitHub Desktop history.
