You have built one site with help and guidance. In this lesson you build a different one on your own, using the same process but a new idea. By the end you will know you can do it without a worked example.

## The challenge

Choose one of these, or pick an idea of your own:

- A page for a club, a team or a small event.
- A menu or price list for a real or imaginary shop.
- A one-page profile of a person you admire.
- A page that explains a hobby to a beginner.

The site should have at least two pages, one section with a list of items, and a layout that works on phones.

## What you will do

1. Create a new empty folder and open it in the assistant.
2. Write a brief with the five parts: goal, who uses it, what it does, what it must not do, and how you will know it is done.
3. Ask the assistant for its questions before it starts, and answer them in the brief.
4. Get the first version, open it, and write a list of what is wrong.
5. Improve it one change at a time, with a commit after each. Make at least five changes.
6. Check it at a narrow width.
7. When something breaks, use the routine you learned: paste the error exactly, say what you expected, and go back to a save point after three failed tries.
8. Go through your "done" checklist by hand and ask the assistant for a self-review.

Use a different framework from the one the assistant chose last time if you like, by asking: `Which tools could you use for this site? Pick the simplest one and explain why.` You do not need to understand the answer in detail. You need to be able to open and check the result.

## Check yourself

- You built a site with at least two pages without copying the earlier one.
- Every change has its own commit, and you handled at least one problem with the error routine.
- You ticked every line of your checklist by looking at the site yourself.

## What comes next

Go on to the course **Publish Your Website**, where you put your site on the internet.
