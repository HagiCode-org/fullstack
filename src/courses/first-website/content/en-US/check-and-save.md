Before you call the site finished, check it against the "done" part of your brief and save the result. In this lesson you do a final check and make a final commit. By the end you will have a site that is verified by you and saved in your history. Putting it on the internet is the next course.

## What you will do

1. Open `brief.md` and copy its "done" lines into a checklist.
2. Open the site and go through the checklist by hand. Tick a line only when you saw it yourself.
3. Check three more things that every site needs: every link works, every page has a heading, and no placeholder text such as "your name here" is left.
4. Check the site at a narrow width one more time.
5. Ask the assistant: `Read the brief and the site files. List anything the brief asks for that is missing, and anything you changed that I did not ask for.` Compare its answer with your own check.
6. Look through the files for anything private, such as a personal email address you do not want public, or a password. If you find one, remove it before you save.
7. Open GitHub Desktop, review the list of changed files, and make a final commit with a description such as `Finished first website`.

## About private information

Everything on a public site can be read by anyone. Only include details you are happy to share with strangers. If a private detail was already saved in an earlier commit, tell the assistant and ask how to remove it properly, because simply deleting it in a new commit leaves it in your history.

## Check yourself

- Every line of your checklist is ticked and you saw each one yourself.
- The site contains no private information and no placeholder text.
- Your final commit appears in GitHub Desktop, and you can name the last commit where the site was complete.

## What comes next

Go on to **Challenge: build a different site**, where you do the whole process again on your own. When you are ready to publish, continue to the course **Publish Your Website**.
