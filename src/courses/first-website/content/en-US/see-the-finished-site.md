In this course you build a personal homepage with an AI partner: a home page, a projects section and a layout that works on phones. In this lesson you look at what the finished site will be, and learn the three words you need to talk about any website. By the end you will know what you are building and how to tell when it is done.

## The concept card

**Page.** One screen of content, such as a home page or an about page. A site is a set of pages that link to each other.

**Browser.** The program you use to look at pages, such as the one you are using now. A browser reads a page and draws it on screen.

**Files.** Every page is stored as a file in a folder. A file named `index.html` is usually the home page. Pictures and style settings are stored as files too. When someone visits a site, the browser asks for these files and shows them.

That is all you need. You do not have to learn how the files are written, because the AI writes them. You do have to know that they exist, because you will open, save and check them.

## What the finished site contains

- A home page with your name, a short introduction and a photo or picture.
- A projects section with three entries, each with a title and a short description.
- A contact line, such as an email address you are happy to share publicly.
- A layout that stays readable on a narrow phone screen.

## What you will do

1. Look at the list above and decide which person the site is about. It can be you, a made-up person or a hobby.
2. Write down three projects you will show. They can be real or invented.
3. Create an empty folder named `first-website` and open it in your AI assistant.
4. Ask the assistant: `In one paragraph, explain what files a small personal website consists of.` Read the answer.

## Check yourself

- You can explain what a page, a browser and a file are.
- You have decided who the site is about and written down three projects.
- You have an empty folder open in your assistant.
