You have now seen three places to publish a website. In this lesson you compare them and choose one for your next project. There is no single best choice: it depends on where your visitors are, and on what you want from the service.

## A short comparison

Cells marked "check" may have changed, so confirm them on the service's own pages.

| | GitHub Pages | Cloudflare | Alibaba Cloud ESA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Effort to start | Lowest: one setting | A second account and a few steps | A second account, and real-name verification |
| Preview link for each change | Not by default | Yes (check) | Check |
| Speed in mainland China | Often slow or unstable | Depends on the network | Designed for mainland China |
| Own domain | You set up DNS yourself | Same service can manage DNS | Mainland-accelerated domains usually need a filing (check) |
| Best for | Personal pages, simple documents | Visitors outside mainland China, or sites that need previews | Visitors in mainland China |

## What you will do

1. Write down where most of your visitors will be.
2. Ask your AI assistant: "My visitors are in this region, and my site is a static site. Which of these three would you suggest, and why?" Treat the answer as advice, not as a decision.
3. Pick one for your next project, and write the reason in one sentence.
4. Note one thing you would check before you pay for anything.

## Check yourself

- You can name one strength and one limit of each service.
- You chose a place for your next project and wrote down why.
