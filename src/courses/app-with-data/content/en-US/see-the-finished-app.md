> **Draft:** this lesson is an outline and will be expanded.

In this course you build a small app that remembers things. It can be a guestbook, where visitors leave a short message, or a bookmark manager, where you save links. The difference from your earlier projects is that this app keeps its information in a **database**, a program that stores data in organized tables so it is still there tomorrow. An AI writes the app, and you decide what it should do and check that it works.

This lesson only looks at the finished result, so you know where you are heading. You do not build anything yet.

**Where the app runs.** Everything in this course runs on your own computer, started with Docker. Putting the back end of an app on a public server so that other people can reach it is not covered here. Your app will be visible only to you.

## What you will do

1. Open the finished example your course page links to and click through it. Add two entries, reload the page, and notice that the entries are still there.
2. Write down, in your own words, what the app lets a person do. For a guestbook it might be "leave a message" and "read all messages".
3. Write down what the app remembers, such as the name, the message and the time it was left.
4. Pick which of the two apps you will build: the guestbook or the bookmark manager. Both follow the same steps.
5. Decide what you want to be different in your version. A different look, an extra field or a different purpose are all fine ideas.

## Check yourself

- You saw an entry stay in the app after you reloaded the page.
- You can say in one sentence what your app lets a person do.
- You know that the app runs only on your computer in this course.
