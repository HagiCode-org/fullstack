A **brief** is a short written description of what you want built. A good brief is the most important thing you give the AI, because it cannot guess what is in your head. In this lesson you write the brief for your guestbook or bookmark manager, including one thing beginners often forget: who is allowed to see and change what.

By the end you have one page of text that you can paste to the AI in the next lesson.

## What you will do

1. Write the purpose in two sentences: who uses the app and what for.
2. List what the app stores. For a guestbook this is the visitor's name, the message and the time. For a bookmark manager it is the title, the link and a note.
3. List what a person can do: add, read, change and delete. Mark each one as needed or optional.
4. Write the access rules in plain words. Answer these questions:
   - Can everyone read all entries, or only the person who made them?
   - Can everyone delete an entry, or only its owner?
   - Does the app need a login at all in this first version?
5. Write what the app must not do, such as "no accounts" or "no emails".
6. Write how you will know it is finished. Use a short checklist, for example "I can add an entry and it is still there after I restart the app".
7. Ask the AI to read your brief and list every question it still has. Answer those questions in the brief.

**Safety note.** Never write a real password, a secret key or any private data in the brief. If the app will need a secret later, write only that it needs one. You handle secrets in the last lessons of this course.

## Check yourself

- Your brief has a purpose, a list of stored items, the actions and the access rules.
- Your brief has a finish checklist you could tick off.
- The AI had no unanswered questions left after you updated the brief.
