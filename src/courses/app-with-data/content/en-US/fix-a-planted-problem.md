Real apps break. In this lesson a problem is planted on purpose in a copy of the app, so that you can practise the habit that matters most when you work with an AI: describe what you see, hand over the evidence, and judge whether the fix is right.

The course page links to a copy of the app with one hidden problem. You do not need to know where it is.

## What you will do

1. Get the practice copy from the link on the course page and start it the same way you started your own app.
2. Use the app until something looks wrong. Typical signs are an entry that does not appear, a button that does nothing or a message saying that something failed.
3. Write down exactly what you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
4. Collect the evidence: the full text of any error shown on the page, and the recent lines of the app's log. Ask the AI to tell you how to show the log of your containers.
5. Give the AI your notes and the evidence, and ask it to find the cause before it changes anything. Read its explanation.
6. Let it make the fix, restart the app and repeat exactly the steps that failed.
7. Check that the rest of the app still works. Ask the AI whether the fix could affect anything else.
8. If the problem is still there after three attempts, go back to the starting point of the practice copy and describe the problem in different words.

## Check yourself

- The step that failed now works.
- The rest of the app still works after the fix.
- You can say in one sentence what the cause was.
- You gave the AI the exact error text and not a paraphrase.
