You have built, extended, fixed and protected one data app. Now you build a different one on your own, with no example to copy. The goal is to prove to yourself that the method works for any small app, not only for the one in this course.

Everything still runs on your own computer. Putting the back end of an app on a public server is not covered in this course.

## What you will do

1. Pick one of these ideas, or one of your own:
   - a reading list with a status for each book
   - a habit tracker that counts days
   - a recipe box with ingredients
   - a small expense log
2. Write the brief as you did before: the purpose, what is stored, what a person can do, who may see or change what, and a finish checklist.
3. Ask the AI to list its questions about the brief and answer them.
4. Save a checkpoint. Then ask for the first version with the database in a container, started by Docker Compose.
5. Run it. Add an entry, restart the app and check that the entry is still there.
6. Add one feature, then check old and new entries.
7. Make a backup before the feature and keep it.
8. Review your work against the finish checklist and write down which part of the method helped you most and which part you would do differently.

## Check yourself

- The app runs from one start command and keeps its entries after a restart.
- No secret was saved to GitHub.
- You made a backup before changing what the app stores.
- You finished without copying an example from the course.
