Your app has its features and you fixed a problem. In this lesson you check it one last time, and then publish it so that anyone can use it. You already know how to publish from the course "Publish Your Website", so this time you do it on your own.

## What you will do

1. Go through your done checklist, one "I can" sentence at a time, on your computer. Then do it again on your phone's screen size, by opening the page on the phone after publishing.
2. Ask your AI assistant to review the project: "Is there anything in this project that should not be public, such as a password, a key or a personal file?" Check the answer yourself too. An app that runs in the browser is public to every visitor.
3. Ask the AI how to build the app into finished files, and what the build command and the output folder are. Write both down.
4. Publish the app. Use the Cloudflare path if you can reach it, or the Alibaba Cloud ESA path if you are in mainland China. Look back at the lessons of the publishing course whenever you need to.
5. Open the published address on another device and run through your checklist again.
6. Share the address with one person, and ask them to try one thing without any help from you. Watch where they get stuck.

## Check yourself

- Every sentence of your done checklist works at the published address.
- You checked that nothing private is in the project.
- Another person used the app without your help.
