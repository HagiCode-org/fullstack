Your app has its features and you fixed a problem. In this lesson you check it one last time, and then publish it so that anyone with the address can use it. You already learned how to publish a website in the publishing course, so this time you do most of the steps yourself.

## What you will do

1. Go through your done checklist, one "I can" sentence at a time, on your computer. After you publish, do it again on a phone-sized screen, by opening the published page on your phone.
2. Ask your AI assistant to review the project: "Is there anything in this project that should not be public, such as a password, a key or a personal file?" Check the answer yourself too. The browser downloads a copy of the page code to the device of whoever opens the address, so do not put secrets in the project's files. [MDN's How the web works article](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn_web_development/Getting_started/Web_standards/How_the_web_works) describes this download.
3. Ask the AI how to build the app into finished files, and what the build command and the output folder are. Write both down. [Cloudflare's build configuration guide](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/configuration/build-configuration/) says that you provide a build command so that Cloudflare Pages knows how to build your application, and that you can say where its output files will be located.
4. Publish the app. If you can reach Cloudflare, use the Cloudflare path. [Cloudflare's Pages documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/) says you can deploy a Pages project by connecting it to a Git provider. If you are in mainland China, use the Alibaba Cloud ESA path instead. Alibaba Cloud's help centre describes [individual identity verification](https://help.aliyun.com/en/account/verify-your-identity-individual-account) for an account, so complete it if Alibaba Cloud asks you to. Look back at the lessons of the publishing course whenever you need to.
5. Open the published address on another device and run through your checklist again.
6. Share the address with one person, and ask them to try one thing without any help from you. Watch where they get stuck, and write it down.

## Why publish against the checklist

A published address is a test of your own description. If a checklist sentence works on your computer but not at the address, the difference is the problem to fix next. Keep the checklist open while you publish, and do not call the app finished until every sentence passes at the address.

## Check yourself

- Every sentence of your done checklist works at the published address.
- You checked that nothing private is in the project.
- Another person used the app without your help.
