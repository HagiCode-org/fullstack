An AI assistant builds what you describe, so a clear description gets a good first version. A **brief** is a short written description of what you want. In this lesson you write the brief for your app, and a checklist that says when the app is done.

## What you will do

1. Write one sentence about who uses the app and why. Example: "I use it to see where my money goes each week."
2. List what the app must do, in short sentences. Keep the list small, five items at most. Example: "I can add an expense with a name, an amount and a date."
3. List what the app does not do yet. Examples: no login, no sharing, no charts. This protects you from a first version that is too big.
4. Write what it should look like, in plain words: simple, readable on a phone, large buttons.
5. Write a done checklist: for each item on your list, one sentence that starts with "I can".
6. Ask your AI assistant: "Here is my brief. What is unclear or missing? Ask me questions before you build anything." Answer its questions and update the brief.
7. Commit the brief to your repository as a file, so you can come back to it.

Do not put real personal data, such as bank details, in your brief or in the examples you give to the AI.

## Check yourself

- Your brief fits on one screen and has a "does not do yet" list.
- Your done checklist has one "I can" sentence for each thing the app does.
- You answered the AI's questions and the brief is saved in your repository.
