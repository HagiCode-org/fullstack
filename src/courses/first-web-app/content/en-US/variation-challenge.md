You have built, fixed and published an app together with an AI assistant. Now do it again, alone, with a different idea. The goal is to see that the method works for any small tool, not only for the one in this course.

## What you will do

1. Choose a small tool you would really use. Some ideas: a habit tracker, a tip splitter, a reading list, a countdown to an event, a unit converter. Keep it small enough to finish in a few hours.
2. Write the brief: who uses it, what it must do, what it does not do yet, and what it should look like.
3. Write the done checklist, one "I can" sentence for each feature.
4. Ask your AI assistant to ask you questions about the brief before it builds anything. Answer them.
5. Build in small steps. After each step, run the app, check it against the checklist, and commit when it works.
6. When something breaks, give the AI the exact error and what you expected. If three tries do not help, go back to your last commit.
7. Publish the tool, and send the address to a friend.

Look at the tools the AI chose for this app, such as the language and the framework, and compare them with the first app. You may find that they are different. The method stays the same: describe, run, check, save.

## Check yourself

- You finished a second tool without following step-by-step instructions.
- Every sentence of its checklist works at the published address.
- You can explain, in your own words, the loop you used: describe, run, check, save.
